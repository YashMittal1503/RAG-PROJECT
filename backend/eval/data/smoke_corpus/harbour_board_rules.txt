Harbour Board Regulations for Lighthouse Keepers (Revised 1930)

1. Watches. The light must be attended at all hours between sunset and sunrise. Keepers shall divide the night into watches of no more than four hours each.

2. Weather log. The weather, wind direction and sea state shall be entered in the station log every four hours, and immediately after any change in the light.

3. Supplies. A supply boat will call at each station once every fourteen days. Requests for oil, wicks and food must be submitted in writing on the previous visit.

4. Leave. Each keeper is entitled to six days of shore leave per quarter, to be taken only when a relief keeper is present at the station.

5. Failure of the light. Any interruption of the light lasting more than ten minutes must be reported to the Harbour Board within forty-eight hours, stating the cause and the remedy taken.

6. Inspection. An inspector of the Harbour Board will visit each station at least once a year and will report on the condition of the apparatus and the conduct of the keepers.
